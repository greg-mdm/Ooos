# Artlist prompt: the Defence clip, one euro and one pound

**Written:** 2026-09-18. For the Toolkit, video-to-video (or the AI Agent
with the clip attached), against
`SPRINT 6/Defence - Defence - Allies are forming.mp4` (9.9 s, 2560x1080,
two gold coins skating backwards side by side, the hockey stop and the
spray at the end). References in `SPRINT 6/coin-refs/`.

## What to attach

1. The Defence clip.
2. `coin-refs/euro-1-common-side-2569px.jpg` (the euro face).
3. `coin-refs/pound-1-2017-reverse-nations-of-the-crown-680px.jpg` (the
   pound face). If the Toolkit wants a larger file, use the 2000px upscale
   beside it.

The 2024 pound reverse (bees) and the Portuguese euro side are spares if
a run reads wrong.

## The prompt

> Keep this video exactly as it is: the same two coins skating backwards
> side by side on the black ice, the same speed, path, spacing, size,
> lighting, teal arena rail lights, reflections, camera and framing, the
> same hockey stop and spray of snow at the end. Change only the design on
> the face of each coin.
>
> The left coin becomes a 1 euro coin, from the first reference image: a
> two-metal coin, silver-white centre with a large "1" and the map of
> Europe, gold outer ring carrying twelve small stars. Round.
>
> The right coin becomes a UK one pound coin, from the second reference
> image: a two-metal coin, twelve-sided, gold outer ring with "ONE POUND"
> along the bottom, silver-white centre with a rose, a leek, a thistle and
> a shamrock rising from a crown.
>
> The two-metal faces catch the same light as the gold did. The engraving
> stays crisp and stays locked to each coin as it turns, skates and stops;
> it never slides, swims or swaps between coins. No other coin appears.
> No text anywhere in the frame beyond what is engraved on the coins. No
> change to the ice, the rink, the snow or the timing.

## Negative

> No extra coins, no coin changing shape mid-shot, no lettering floating
> off the coin, no flicker in the engraving, no colour shift in the arena,
> no slow motion, no cut, no zoom.

## Notes for the run

- The euro's silver centre is the read; if a run leaves the coins all
  gold, say so in the follow-up: "the euro's centre is silver-white, the
  ring is gold; the pound's ring is gold, the centre silver-white."
- The pound's twelve sides are the second read. If the model keeps it
  round, ask for the twelve straight edges by name.
- If the faces swim, ask for the engraving to be "locked to the coin as a
  decal" and drop the strength of the change one notch.
- Left and right are as the viewer sees them. Swap the two sentences if
  Greg wants the pound on the left.
