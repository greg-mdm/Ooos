## Main prompt (paste this)

A single photoreal gold coin, the Toronto skyline coin with the CN Tower on
its face, skates on its edge like a figure skater across a minimalist scene
of angular concrete planes: cool desaturated teal-green-grey, lit like an
architectural render with soft ambient light and a faint purple atmospheric
glow at the top. The foreground wedge the coin skates on is black ice:
deep midnight blue-black, glassy, with faint cold reflections of the coin and
the planes above. The coin enters from the left at speed, carves three long
elegant edges, spins twice on its rim with real weight and finesse, then
stops short in the centre of the frame with a hockey stop: a hard sideways
check that throws a fast, brilliant white spray of snow forward and upward,
luminous where the sunlight catches it, crystalline, natural, settling
quickly. The coin keeps all of its gold, every engraved line sharp, warm
golden light on its rim, a soft contact shadow and a faint cool glow on the
ice beneath it. The camera is locked, wide, eye level with the planes.
Cinematic, sovereign, calm, premium, editorial. Ends holding on the coin at
rest with the last flecks of snow drifting down. 10 seconds.

## Two shorter variants, if the first reads busy

**Tight.** A photoreal gold Toronto skyline coin skates on its rim across
dark blue-black ice between cool grey concrete planes, carves two elegant
edges, spins twice, and stops short with a hockey stop that sprays brilliant
white snow forward into the sunlight. The coin stays fully gold. Locked
camera, architectural lighting, editorial calm. Ends at rest. 10 seconds.

**Physics first.** Locked wide shot. A gold coin on its edge crosses black
ice from left to centre, decelerates through a rounded curve, spins once,
then bites the ice sideways and stops dead in the middle of the frame. The
stop throws a quick fan of white snow forward and up, lit by the sun, then
settles. Concrete planes above in teal-grey, faint purple glow at the top.
Nothing else moves. 10 seconds.
