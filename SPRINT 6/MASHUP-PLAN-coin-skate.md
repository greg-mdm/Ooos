## The performance beat, shots 4 to 6 (revised 2026-09-17)

Greg's direction: not a practice session in an empty arena but a lit
ice-dance number with the production of a music video. Multi-camera cutting
so the eye never sits level on the coin long enough to read a smear, and
the arena treated as a Eurovision stage: strobes, colour washes, rhythmic
light changes cut to the beat, which also explain any softness as light.

Rules for the stretch: no cut over 2 seconds, no straight-on view of the
coin over 1.5 seconds, a light change on every cut, a two-frame white
flash on every cut from 4a to 6. Pick the tempo before cutting and place
the strobes on its grid; the music is swapped under it later.

Studio lighting: stock overlays (search "strobe overlay", "light leak",
"concert lighting", "stage beams") on a second track, blend Screen or
Add, 40 to 70 percent. Tints: purple on 4, teal on 5a, magenta on 5b and
5c, gold on 6.

## The finale, revised 2026-09-17: timelapse and the art attack

Greg's direction: the aerial shot shows the coin carving a circle into the
ice, so the routine does not end on a stop. It runs on and speeds up, a
timelapse of carving, spinning, rolling and skating backwards, and the
finale reveals the art attack from above: the Ooo! wordmark carved in the
centre of the circle.

The wordmark on the ice:
- The bubble letters are **filled**, not outlined. Ooo! never reads as
  empty; the coin has carved shading into the whole face of each letter.
- Bright silver-white on the dark ice, the way the brand's silver wordmark
  sits on indigo.
- No logo, no depth, no extrusion. Flat carving, read by light.
- Three colours of light hit it from three sides, and the shading answers
  each one, so the letters look right under all three.
- Reference: Greg's best render of the silver-white Ooo! shading on dark
  ice. It has not reached this folder yet; save it here as
  `WORDMARK-ICE-REF.png` and it becomes the end frame below. Letterforms
  come from `public/assets/brand/ooo-wordmark-white-transparent.png` (or
  the silver on transparent), never redrawn.

## Faces: what holds and what has to be rebuilt

The brief: treat each face as a green screen locked to `skyline.png`
(front) and `wolf.png` (back) whenever a face is visible; when a spin is too
fast to carry a face, the coin goes blank, polished, reflective gold.

Artlist cannot lock a face onto footage that already exists. There is no
tracking or replace step in the Toolkit or Studio. Two routes, and the plan
uses both:

1. **Keep the takes where the face already holds.** Shots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8
   and 9 show the skyline cleanly or show the coin on its edge. They need
   nothing.
2. **Regenerate the two shots where it fails**, with the coin registered as
   a Character in the Toolkit (built from `skyline.png` and `wolf.png`) and
   the arena plate as the start frame, so the generator draws the right
   faces rather than an editor pasting them on:
   - Shot 4, the spotlight spin: the face is readable, but the cloud after
     it is a spray without a stop. Regenerate only if the spin reads soft
     at full size.
   - Shot 7, Defence: the centre coin smears at 6 s. Regenerate with the
     blank-gold rule in the prompt. This is the one that matters.

### Prompt: Defence, regenerated (Seedance 2.5, 10 s, 21:9, 1080p)

Start frame: `ARENA - SHOT2.png`. Characters: @skyline coin, @wolf coin.

Three photoreal gold coins skate backwards in a tight defensive formation
across black ice inside a glass dome arena lit by teal rail lights, the
camera locked at ice level. Two coins face the camera and hold their faces
perfectly still and sharp through the whole move: the left coin shows the
wolf, the right coin shows the Toronto skyline with the CN Tower. The
centre coin skates on its edge and spins fast; while it spins it is blank,
polished, mirror-bright gold with no engraving, reflecting the rail lights.
They glide backwards together, cross once, and reform. No stop, no spray.
Warm gold, cool arena, editorial calm. No text, no logos, no extra coins,
no blur on the two faces.

### Prompt: the spin, regenerated if needed (Seedance 2.5, 6 s)

Start frame: `ARENA - SHOT2.png`. Character: @skyline coin.

A single photoreal gold Toronto skyline coin spins slowly on its rim at
centre ice under two indigo-purple spotlights in a glass dome arena. The
spin is slow enough that the CN Tower face reads on every pass; between
passes the rim catches the light. Locked camera at ice level, black ice
reflections. No stop, no snow, no text.

### The blank-gold rule, for any future generation

When a coin turns faster than a face can be read, it is rendered as blank,
polished, reflective gold: no engraving, no lettering, a mirror of the
arena. The face returns only when the coin slows. This is a design rule,
not a fallback: unrealised possibility, a blank slate.
