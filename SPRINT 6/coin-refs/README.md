# Coin references for the Defence clip

Reference stills for the Artlist run that turns the two coins in
`Defence - Defence - Allies are forming.mp4` into a 1 euro and a UK 1 pound.
Gathered 2026-09-18. These are inputs to the generator, not assets for the
site; nothing here is published on ooos.ca.

The common side (the big 1, the map, the twelve stars) is the face that
reads as "euro" at a glance; use it as the primary reference. The Portuguese
side is a spare in case the model wants a national side too. The common-side
photo shows the 2002 map (EU15); the 2007 map differs only in the
coastline and the model will not care.

No larger current-design common-side photo exists on Commons; the ECB's own
images are 540 px.

The 2017 reverse ("ONE POUND", the rose, leek, thistle and shamrock in a
crown) is the face that reads as "pound" at a glance; use it as the primary
reference, with the 2024 reverse (the bees, the large 1 POUND) as a spare.
The 12-sided shape and the two-metal look are what carry the read.

There is no openly licensed high-resolution photograph of the 12-sided
coin: the design is Crown copyright and Commons removes them. The Royal
Mint's product photography is the best available and is theirs.

## Rights, in one line

Both coin designs are protected (European Commission; Royal Mint / Crown).
Using photographs of them to steer a generator, and showing a stylised
coin skating on ice in a short film, is the same territory as any film that
shows money on screen. Nothing here reproduces a coin as a coin.
