# Micro De Luxe

Raw media folder for Micro De Luxe, the mascot of Media, Information and Culture (MIC). Opened 2026-09-24.

Upload the character art and the character videos here as they are, at full size. Nothing in this folder is served by the site; it is the source library. Web-ready cuts are made from these files and installed under `public/assets/brand/`.

## What goes here

- Character art: the finished renders and any alternate poses, as PNG at full resolution.
- Character videos: the mascot clips as delivered (MP4 preferred).
- Working notes, prompts and reference images for the character, if any.

## Homepage plan

- The hero render replaces the MIC emcee icon in the division card on the homepage (`public/assets/brand/mic-emcee-640.webp`).
- The videos are held here until a placement on the homepage is decided.

## Notes

- GitHub refuses single files over 100 MB. Keep each video under that, or split a long clip.
- Keep the original file names on upload; the web-ready copies get their own names when they are cut.
