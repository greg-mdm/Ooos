# Forest parallax (reference)

Source of the CodePen "Forest parallax" by Andrej Sharapov, downloaded 2026-10-05 as the reference for the forest backdrop behind the Vivarium tower on the CID page.

- Pen: https://codepen.io/andrejsharapov/pen/joZzEV
- Created and last updated: May 23, 2019
- Licence: MIT, as shown on the pen's details page (see `LICENSE.txt`; the year in its copyright line is the one CodePen printed on the day of download)
- Files: `index.pug`, `style.scss`, `script.js`, exactly as in the pen's three editors. The pen also loads jQuery.

## What the site uses from it

The idea only: a forest that lives behind the content with the ground at its foot. The site's version is one looping video in React and CSS (`src/app/components/ForestBackdrop.tsx`, `.cid-viv-forest` in `src/styles/cid-vivarium.css`), no jQuery and no parallax layers.

## What the site does not use: the pictures

The pen's five pictures are not Sharapov's. The pen credits them to https://tornis.robbowen.digital/, where Robb Owen describes them as his own composite images based on Unsplash photographs by Nicole Harrington, Krists Luhaers and Filip Zrnzevic. Tornis the library is MIT-licensed on GitHub, but that repository holds only the library, not these pictures, so nothing grants a licence to reuse them. They are hot-linked in the pen and are **not** copied into this repository or onto the site. The site first drew its own fir layers as placeholders (2026-10-05); on 2026-10-06 they were retired for Greg's own clip, `SPRINT 6/PHTALO FOREST VIDEO.mp4`, prepared as a slow seamless loop at `public/assets/video/cid-phthalo-forest-loop.mp4`.
