The Angry Boy

Gustav Vigeland’s little bronze Angry Boy (Sinnataggen) has been stamping his foot on the bridge in Frogner Park since 1940. He is Oslo’s most famous tantrum.

The stomping baby in our banner is a street-art copy, spray-painted near Holmenkollen, the ski jump from the 1952 Winter Olympics.
