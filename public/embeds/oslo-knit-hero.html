See how the feminine shapes are nested? Each deep V-shape stays sharp as the chevrons are delicately drawn into the repeating pattern. No squiggly strands or tangled yarn.

Arty Fact: Access Granted

A chevron is a V-shaped symbol used in heraldry, a tradition in which artefacts are created and coats of arms are formally granted.

Artistic Director: Gregory Tyler Long

Tagged: Sinnataggen (The Angry Boy)

Medium: Spray-paint on a concrete slab

This one-of-a-kind artifact is attributed to Norwegian street artist La Staa.

Habitat: Holmenkollen Mountain

Type status: Holotype (single, site-specific object). Seemingly left here by La Staa.

Mimicry class: Mimesis. The concrete impression represents Vigeland’s Sinnataggen.

Oslo’s most famous tantrum. Originally sculpted in bronze by Gustav Vigeland, this little boy has been stamping his foot on the bridge in Frogner Park since 1940.
