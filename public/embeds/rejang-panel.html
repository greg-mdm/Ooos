꥟ Rejang Section Mark

The Indigenous Rejang people of Sumatra originally carved this intricate symbol into bamboo to mark a place to pause. We appreciate the aesthetic design while honouring its origin.

What does the Rejang section mark look like to you?

Answer: It is a functional punctuation mark. While the possibilities are fun to imagine, it officially opens and closes sections in Aksara Kaganga (Rejang script), recognized by the Indonesian Ministry of Education and Culture as an Intangible Cultural Heritage (Warisan Budaya Takbenda Indonesia, Decree No. 238/M/2013).
